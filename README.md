# Hi, I'm Santiago Balbarrey 👋

### Data Analyst · BI Developer · AgTech

I spent 8+ years running a mixed crop and cattle operation in southern Buenos Aires province, Argentina, leading a team of 6 and making day-to-day decisions on crop rotations, input costs and herd performance. I also studied Agronomy at university.

Today I turn that field experience into data products: ETL pipelines, data models and dashboards that help people make better decisions.

📍 Based in Berlin · Open to Data Analyst / BI roles, especially in AgTech
